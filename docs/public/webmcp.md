# Tesla Workbench WebMCP surface

The workbench exposes ten calculation and validation tools plus two SecuredMe companion tools from `core.webmcp`. Every callable is backed by an existing source-grounded primitive or an explicitly staged payload.

The surface does not control physical equipment, optimize a transmitter, support weaponization, or certify causal physical claims. Fractal NeutroGeometry outputs preserve `I -> I_system^S -> D_f -> dF -> i_fractal` and remain modeled or hypothesis-layer readings under human review.

The companion uses the product-specific Stitch design document as the visual source of truth and falls back to accessible SecuredMe tokens when no dedicated asset is available.
